Featured Novel

A Question of Balance

A literary psychological suspense novel.

Dr. Steven Gold has built his life on understanding the mind—his patients’ and his own. Then Samael Light enters his Wilshire Boulevard office and calmly claims to be Satan.

Gold dismisses the claim as delusion. But as their sessions deepen, Light begins exposing fractures in Gold’s marriage, his beliefs, and his carefully constructed sense of self.

When Rabbi Aaron Shapiro is found murdered after a public confrontation with Gold, the questions raised inside the consulting room become part of a criminal investigation. As suspicion grows and certainty gives way to doubt, Gold must decide whether Light is deluded, dangerous—or telling a truth Gold is not prepared to hear.

The novel is complete at approximately 95,000 words and is currently seeking literary representation.

Author

David S. Sherman, Ph.D., is a California-licensed psychotherapist, former graduate psychology instructor, technology entrepreneur, and novelist.

He earned his B.A. from Harvard, his M.A. from USC, and his Ph.D. from UCLA. His fiction draws on decades of clinical experience to explore identity, guilt, belief, consciousness, and moral responsibility.

A Question of Balance is his debut novel.
